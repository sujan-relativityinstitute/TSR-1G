## Historical Inertia Summary

Tunisia's H field at the 1990 baseline carries the following layers:

- **Ottoman legacy and French Protectorate (1881-1956):** Deep center-periphery
  imprint. Coastal (Tunis, Sfax, Sousse) administrative and economic primacy
  embedded in infrastructure and institutional design. Interior regions
  structurally subordinated. This gradient did not disappear at independence;
  it was inherited and amplified.

- **Bourguiba era (1956-1987):** Secular nationalist state-building.
  Strong O_ord through single-party (Destour/PSD) structure. High investment
  in education and health (E_m relatively high for a developing economy in
  this period). Women's rights modernization increased x3 divergence from
  conservative religious populations. Created the educated graduate class
  whose unemployment would later become the critical E'/E" divergence proxy.

- **Ben Ali coup (November 7, 1987):** Presented as constitutional correction
  of Bourguiba's declining capacity. Initially raised L briefly. Quickly
  transitioned to consolidation of D_dom. The date Nov 7 ("le Changement")
  was heavily mythologized in regime narrative — a Psi anchor that became
  increasingly brittle as reality diverged.

- **Islamist suppression (1989-1992):** Ennahda movement suppressed after
  strong 1989 election performance. Created a large politically active
  population stored at high T" toward the Sol on both x2 and x3. This
  population's grievances entered the Omega field and remained latent
  throughout the Ben Ali period. Represents a major component of stored
  stress not visible in standard economic indicators.

- **IMF structural adjustment (1986-1994):** Economic opening reduced state
  employment guarantees. Began the widening of the x1 distribution and
  the accumulation of the informal labor sector (D_dissipation weakening
  as formal channels contracted).

## Key Structural Transitions

**1995-2000: Coastal growth / interior divergence**
EU Association Agreement accelerated FDI into coastal export zones. Interior
regions received minimal benefit. PI_w/PI_n ratio begins rising. The
coastal-interior split is the primary x1 polydispersity driver and the
geographic expression of the elite/mass temperature gradient.

**2000-2003: D_dom peak**
Constitutional manipulation (2002), anti-terror law (2001), peak political
imprisonment. A_aut at minimum. O_ord captured fully by Sol. L nominally
stable but structurally hollow. Omega accumulating in peripheral and
Islamist-adjacent populations.

**2005: WSIS contradiction**
World Summit on Information Society hosted in Tunis while internet was
heavily censored. International exposure of the gap between official
narrative and manifest reality. First major Q_coupling erosion event
visible to external actors.

**2008: Gafsa — the non-trigger**
Gafsa mining basin protests (January-June 2008) demonstrate that high
Omega alone is insufficient for system-wide cascade. C_conn between
interior protest region and coastal population was too weak (pre-Facebook
threshold). Content vector primarily x1-loaded (mining employment) without
strong x2 or x3 resonance in coastal populations. Mode 4 amplification
occurred locally but propagation reach was near zero nationally.
Global financial crisis compounded E_economic_stress in Q3-Q4 2008.

**2009-2010: Surface transformation**
Graduate unemployment reaches maximum (E'/E" divergence peak). Facebook
penetration crosses threshold enabling C_conn between interior and coast.
Arab Barometer Wave 2 captures L and T' in freefall relative to Wave 1.
Theta_majority rising while Theta_elite stable: TG_hazard widening.
WikiLeaks Cablegate (Nov 28 2010) raises T" toward Sol in digitally
connected urban populations — surface pre-conditioning for amplification.

**Dec 17 2010: Bouazizi — the trigger**
High symbolic criticality: simultaneous x1 (market vendor denied livelihood),
x2 (arbitrary state power, public humiliation by female official), x3
(generational dignity, regional identity, body-as-protest). G_stored maximum.
C_conn sufficient for propagation (Facebook, Al Jazeera, mobile). Mode 4
amplification produced energy release far exceeding the signal's initial
intensity. Narrative synchronization cascade followed.
