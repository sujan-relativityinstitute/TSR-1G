## Key calibration anchors

**Gafsa 2008 (TUN-2008-Q1) vs. Bouazizi 2010 (TUN-2010-W50)**
These two events are the primary calibration pair for symbolic criticality and
propagation reach parameters. Omega load was high in both cases. The 2008 event
remained local because internal Connection was insufficient (pre-Facebook
threshold) and multi-axis coupling was lower. The 2010 event achieved Mode 4
amplification and system-wide cascade. Modeling both precisely tests whether
the framework correctly predicts differential outcomes from similar triggers.

**Ben Ali re-election results as L proxy**
- 1994: 99.9% — L at nominal maximum (coerced, not real)
- 1999: 99.4%
- 2004: 94.5%
- 2009: 89.6% — declining even within managed results
The declining margin is a detectable signal of L erosion even through a captured
electoral system. Use as a longitudinal L anchor for calibration.

**WikiLeaks Cablegate (Nov 28 2010)**
Not a shock event. Code as a separate inforay in tsr_inforay_events:
origin = external/international, content_v2 high (political: regime corruption
exposed), T_prime_toward_origin moderate (Al Jazeera as transmission vector
had moderate-to-high T' in outer orbit populations). Effect: raised T" toward
Sol across connected urban populations. Warmed the surface for Bouazizi
amplification 19 days later.

**Military refusal to fire (mid-January 2011)**
Code as a separate inforay in tsr_inforay_events: origin = inside Configuration
(military institution), content_v2 high, T_prime_toward_origin very high
(military distinguished from police by population). Interaction mode:
constructive absorption for opposition narrative. Triggered final L collapse
and Political Singularity.
