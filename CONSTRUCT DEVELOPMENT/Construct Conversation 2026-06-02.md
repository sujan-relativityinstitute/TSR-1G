## Topic 2: GR as a Scenario-Driven Equation -- Social Analogs

### Question
GR seems to be a very simple container, with a clean equation, but there are so many aspects of this -- very deep, and solving it in various scenarios gives various predictions and views into aspects of matter in spacetime. Explain how that works and what could be the social analogs.

### Core Principle

Einstein's field equation is a relationship, not a recipe. The curvature of spacetime (left side) equals the distribution of matter and energy (right side). Those two sides are always in balance. What changes between scenarios is not the equation -- it is the symmetry assumptions, boundary conditions, and matter configuration brought to the equation. The equation is the constraint. The scenario is what you bring. The solution is what falls out.

### GR Scenarios and Their Social Analogs

**1. Schwarzschild Solution (isolated, spherically symmetric mass)**
GR: All mass at a point, perfect spherical symmetry, nothing else. Produces: event horizon, time dilation, geodesic orbits.
TSR analog -- Isolated Economic Singularity: Pure wealth concentration, no ideological rotation, no historical complexity. Produces: an accumulation horizon (threshold beyond which capital returns to capital regardless of actor intent -- self-sustaining, not chosen); time distortion (compressed time horizons near the singularity -- short-termism as geometry, not character flaw); geodesic trajectories of actors at various distances following their respective curvature.

**2. Kerr Solution (rotating black hole)**
GR: Adds angular momentum to the Schwarzschild configuration. Produces: frame dragging (rotation of the singularity drags spacetime around it), ergosphere (region where nothing can remain stationary regardless of effort).
TSR analog -- Rotating Political Sol + Economic Singularity: When the political structure has acquired angular momentum (ideological momentum, historical inertia rotating the manifold), frame dragging means the powerful literally drag social space around them -- norms, laws, narratives rotated by proximity to power. The ergosphere analog is a region where actors cannot maintain a stationary position relative to the rest of society no matter what they do. They are dragged along. This is not a metaphor for political capture; it is a geometric prediction.

**3. FLRW Solution (cosmological expansion)**
GR: Assumes homogeneity and isotropy at large scales. Produces: expanding universe, metric itself changing over time.
TSR analog -- Macro-historical manifold dynamics: At civilizational and centennial scales, the social manifold itself expands or contracts. Globalization expanding configuration space. Technological disruption stretching the manifold. A collapsing regime compressing it. The question of whether a society is "opening" or "closing" is a question about the metric, not about the people.

**4. Gravitational waves (perturbations propagating through spacetime)**
GR: Linearized perturbations to flat spacetime that propagate at the speed of light. Two massive objects spiraling toward merger produce ripples.
TSR analog -- Information and crisis shocks: Financial crises, revolutions, and information shocks propagate through the social manifold as disturbances that carry energy without carrying matter. The narrative field Ψ is the medium through which these disturbances travel. The theory, if properly formalized, predicts propagation speed, attenuation, and which configurations are transparent vs. absorbing.

**5. Tidal tensor (geodesic deviation)**
GR: Describes differential curvature across the extent of a body. Two freely falling particles that start side by side converge or diverge depending on curvature. Near a black hole, the difference in pull across an extended body tears it apart (spaghettification).
TSR analog -- Class stratification as geometry: Two actors who start at similar positions but at different distances from the Economic Singularity diverge in trajectory over time, not because of different choices but because the curvature gradient acts differently on them. The gradient steepens closer in. This is why the 0.01% behaves qualitatively differently from the 1% -- not quantitatively more of the same thing, but different in kind, because the tidal field at that distance reorganizes perception, time horizon, social relationship, and information environment in ways that do not apply at larger distances. This is the mechanism of class stratification as a geometric phenomenon, not a moral one.

**6. Gravitational lensing (bending of null geodesics)**
GR: Light follows null geodesics, bent by curvature. A massive object between observer and source bends the light, making the source appear displaced.
TSR analog -- Information lensing: Information and narrative travel along null-geodesic analogs through the social manifold. The Economic Singularity and Political Sol bend these paths. What an actor far from the singularity sees is not the raw phenomenon; it is the lensed image, bent by every concentration of influence between them and the source. Propaganda works not because people are gullible but because it exploits a real geometric feature of the manifold. The distortion follows the geometry of where concentrations are and how strong they are.

### Unifying Point

None of these scenarios require a separate theory. They all require the same governing equation applied to different configurations. This is exactly why the GR architecture is the right one for TSR: because social reality has many faces -- revolutions, crises, capture, normative collapse, information warfare -- and a theory requiring a separate model for each is not a theory. It is a collection of descriptions. TSR-1G aspires to be the one equation from which all of those faces fall out as solutions.
